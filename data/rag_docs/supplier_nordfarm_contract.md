# Synthetic Supplier Agreement — NordFarm Poultry GmbH

**Supplier:** SUP-NORD  
**Relevant SKUs:** CHK-250, CHK-250-B

## 4. Standard delivery

Standard purchase orders require five business days of lead time unless another delivery date has been confirmed in writing.

## 5. Expedited supply

For material shortages, the buyer may request expedited supply with at least 48 hours notice. NordFarm may provide up to 1,200 additional units per calendar week through the expedited process, subject to available capacity. Expedited quantities carry an 8% unit-price surcharge. An expedited request is not considered confirmed until NordFarm returns a written confirmation.

## 6. Delivery reliability

A confirmed delivery date represents the supplier's committed delivery date. If a confirmed delivery is expected to miss the committed date, NordFarm must notify the buyer as soon as practical.

## 7. Alternate SKU

SKU CHK-250-B is produced under the same supplier quality program but may only be used where the buyer's internal substitution policy explicitly approves it.
