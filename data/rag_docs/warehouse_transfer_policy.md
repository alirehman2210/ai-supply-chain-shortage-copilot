# Synthetic Inter-Fulfillment-Center Transfer Policy

**Document type:** transfer_policy

## Transfer eligibility

A fulfillment center may transfer an ingredient to another site only if the sending site retains enough projected inventory to satisfy its own confirmed forecast for the same planning date.

## Timing

Standard inter-site transfer time between German fulfillment centers is 24 hours once approved and dispatched.

## Capacity

A single urgent transfer request may contain up to 1,000 units of a temperature-controlled ingredient such as chilled chicken.

## Approval

Urgent transfers require approval from the responsible supply-chain planner before dispatch. The system may recommend or draft a transfer but may not execute it automatically.
