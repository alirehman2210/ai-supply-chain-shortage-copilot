# Synthetic Supply Shortage Response SOP

**Document type:** shortage_sop

When a projected shortage is detected, planners should evaluate mitigation in this order when practical:

1. Verify the demand and inventory data.
2. Confirm purchase orders expected before the production cutoff.
3. Evaluate safe inventory transfers from other fulfillment centers.
4. Evaluate pre-approved ingredient substitutions.
5. Evaluate supplier expedite options.
6. Escalate unresolved shortages that could affect customer deliveries.

Recommendations should show the remaining shortage after each proposed action. No physical transfer, supplier request, or production-plan change should be executed without the appropriate human approval.
