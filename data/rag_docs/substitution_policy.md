# Synthetic Ingredient Substitution Policy

**Document type:** substitution_policy  
**Primary SKU:** CHK-250  
**Alternate SKU:** CHK-250-B

## Principle

Substitution is allowed only when the alternate SKU has been pre-approved for the specific recipe and the substitution does not create an undeclared allergen, nutrition, sourcing, or portion-size change.

## Approved uses

`CHK-250-B` is approved as a direct substitute for `CHK-250` in:

- Chicken Teriyaki (`RCP-TERIYAKI`)
- Tuscan Chicken (`RCP-TUSCAN`)

`CHK-250-B` is **not approved** for Creamy Chicken Pasta (`RCP-PASTA`) because the current alternate-SKU preparation specification has not been validated for that recipe's sauce process.

## Quantity constraint

A fulfillment center may use available approved alternate inventory up to the physical on-hand quantity. The substitution must be recorded for traceability.
